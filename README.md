 UNP_VAC_PROJECT_GRANDHORIZON_HOSPITALITY_REVENUE_ANAIYTICS_USING_MS_EXCEL

The GrandHorizon Hospitality Revenue Analytics project, completed in collaboration with the United Network of Professionals, focused on transforming hospitality data into meaningful business insights. We performed data cleaning and preprocessing, handled missing values and duplicates, identified outliers, and applied statistical techniques including hypothesis testing, correlation, regression, variance analysis, ANOVA, and A/B testing. We also created pivot tables, charts, and visualizations to understand revenue and operational performance. The objective was to identify important patterns, relationships, and factors affecting hotel performance and support better business decisions.
